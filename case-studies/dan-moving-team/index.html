Dan The Moving Man

Turning moving-company support into a managed operating rhythm

Managing a VA team, assigning ownership, monitoring completion, coaching individuals, resolving claims and standardising work through SOPs and Loom tutorials.

Current role · verified responsibilities
Operating storyContextProblemOwnershipChangeEvidence

Context

The operating environment

A moving-company environment where customer communication, sales follow-up, inventories, CRM records and field issues must move together. Jevi returned for a second stint as VA Manager on 30 March 2026, working flexibly for approximately 15 hours per week.

Problem

The friction or visibility gap

Daily work crossed several people and channels. Without clear ownership and repeatable guidance, tasks could require repeated follow-up, decisions could vary, and customer disputes could remain open longer than necessary.

What Jevi owned

Decisions, people, workflow and follow-through

  • VA-team coordination and day-to-day work allocation
  • Delegation, completion monitoring and follow-up
  • Individual coaching and practical performance support
  • Customer dispute and claims coordination
  • SOPs, operational guides, training material and Loom tutorials
  • Customer and sales communication when the operation required it

Diagnosis

What was actually getting in the way

The core risk was not a lack of activity. It was variation: the same type of work could be handled differently depending on who received it, while the owner or manager remained the default escalation point.

Changes made

How the operating model changed

  • Made task ownership explicit and followed work through to completion
  • Converted recurring explanations into written standards and short video guidance
  • Used individual coaching to correct execution gaps
  • Kept customer, sales and field context connected to the team workflow

Standard introduced

What became repeatable

A managed rhythm of assign, confirm, monitor, coach and close—supported by SOPs and Loom tutorials rather than one person’s memory.

Systems used

Moving-company CRM records, customer communication channels, inventory and estimate context, operating guides and Loom.

Result / evidence

What can be stated publicly

The public claim is the verified scope of the current VA Manager role. No team-size or performance metric is published because none has been confirmed for this role.

What became easier

The team has a clearer source of direction, recurring work is easier to repeat consistently, and escalations have an accountable owner.
