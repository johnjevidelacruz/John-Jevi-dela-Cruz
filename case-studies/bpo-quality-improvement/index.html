Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) | Lead Quality Analyst

Turning quality findings into coaching and measurable improvement

Team leadership, calibration, reporting and targeted performance follow-through.

Context

The situation

A customer-support quality programme where audit quality, coaching consistency and leadership reporting had to operate as one system. Jevi served as Lead Quality Analyst from July 2023 to January 2025.

My role

My role in the work

  • Leadership of 10 quality analysts
  • Audit and coaching schedules supporting approximately 25 agents
  • Weekly calibration within the team and across sites/partners
  • Power BI extraction, charts, insight decks and business reviews
  • QA guideline rollout, root-cause coaching and action planning

The control point

Quality data must lead to a specific action

I grouped findings into clear failure modes so the team could distinguish isolated misses from recurring behaviour or process issues.

What I changed

What I changed

  • Standardised scoring through weekly calibration
  • Focused higher-frequency support on five bottom performers
  • Connected QA findings to training and Operations action plans
  • Presented concise evidence to clients and management

Evidence

10 QAs led and measurable movement in performance

I supported approximately 25 agents through roughly 30 audits and 30 coaching sessions each week. Empathy non-compliance moved from 60% to 45% over three weeks, while average handling time moved from 685 to 618 seconds during the supported improvement period.
