Moving Company | VA Manager

Leading a remote team and making the work easier to repeat

Delegation, task monitoring, coaching, dispute resolution, SOPs and Loom training.

Context

A distributed team supporting a live moving operation

Customer communication, sales follow-up, CRM records, estimates and field issues all move at the same time. I returned to the company as VA Manager in March 2026 after an earlier operations support role.

What I own

The people and the operating rhythm

  • Delegate work and monitor completion
  • Coach individuals and follow up on execution gaps
  • Resolve disputes, claims and customer escalations
  • Create SOPs, operating guides, training material and Loom tutorials

How I operate

Assign, monitor, coach and close

I make ownership explicit, follow work through and turn recurring explanations into guidance the team can reuse. Customer, sales and field context stay connected to the task instead of being split across inboxes and individual memory.

What this demonstrates

Management with systems behind it

The role combines day-to-day leadership with the documentation and training needed to make execution more consistent.
