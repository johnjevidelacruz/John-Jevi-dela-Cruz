Case study

Support Vendor: Lifted Empathy Compliance While Reducing AHT

Empathy behaviors are often treated as ‘soft’ and hard to improve without increasing AHT. This effort targeted a single behavior, coached it systematically, and measured change while AHT improved.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Lead QA / coaching partner
  • Stack: QA scorecards, coaching plans, Power BI extracts
  • Deliverables: Coaching plan, training intervention, weekly tracking

Key highlights

  • Behavior-specific coaching (empathy)
  • Measured impact with weekly tracking
  • AHT improved alongside compliance

Context

A support program struggled with empathy behaviors while also being sensitive to handle time (AHT).

Objective

Increase empathy compliance without increasing AHT—ideally improving both by changing agent behavior efficiently.

Baseline

Week 0 empathy non-compliance: 60% (n=578). AHT: 685 seconds.

Approach

I ran a diagnostic coaching loop: identify patterns, teach replacements, and verify movement week over week.

  • Reviewed failures and grouped them into common patterns (missing acknowledgement, tone, phrasing).
  • Implemented coaching + word-substitution training to reduce cognitive load for agents.
  • Tracked compliance movement weekly and checked AHT trends to avoid unintended tradeoffs.

Results

By week 3: empathy non-compliance dropped to 45% (compliance 40% → 55%, +15pp) while AHT decreased from 685s to 618s (~-9.8%).

Why it worked

The intervention reduced ambiguity: agents didn’t need to “be more empathetic” in general—they had specific, repeatable phrases and behaviors to use.

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Turn a vague behavior (empathy) into observable micro-behaviors.
  • Use word-substitution training to reduce effort and improve consistency.
  • Track both quality and productivity together to avoid hidden regressions.
  • Re-audit after coaching to verify change.
