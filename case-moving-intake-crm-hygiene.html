Case study

Moving Company: Lead Intake + CRM Hygiene to Stop Lead Leaks

A moving company was receiving leads across calls, voicemails, email, and SMS—without a consistent intake standard. The result was predictable: missing details, duplicate leads, and slow follow-up.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Ops Support / CRM Specialist
  • Stack: Supermove CRM, RingCentral, email/SMS, QuickBooks Time
  • Deliverables: Intake SOP + CRM stage design, tag taxonomy, handoff rules, basic pipeline reporting

Key highlights

  • Minimum-viable intake standard (no overengineering)
  • CRM stages aligned to the real sales process
  • Daily QA pass for duplicates + missing fields

Context

Moving teams often lose revenue not because leads are scarce, but because intake is inconsistent and the CRM becomes untrustworthy.

Objective

Standardize intake and CRM hygiene so every lead has complete details, a next action, and a clear owner—without adding friction to the team.

Starting point

Leads arriving via call/email/SMS with missing fields, duplicated records, inconsistent tags, and unclear handoffs.

Approach

I rebuilt the intake workflow around “minimum required info” and simple rules that are easy to follow under pressure.

  • Defined a minimum dataset for quoting/booking (move date, addresses, inventory notes, access constraints, contact info).
  • Aligned statuses and tags to real operational stages (not “whatever someone typed”).
  • Added a definition of done for intake: owner assigned + next action scheduled.
  • Created a weekly hygiene cadence: dedupe, missing fields, stale leads follow-up.

Deliverables

A practical CRM operating model.

  • Intake templates/scripts for call, email, and SMS.
  • CRM stage/tag definitions and required fields.
  • Handoff checklist for sales/dispatch/ops.
  • Weekly KPI snapshot + hygiene checklist.

Results

Follow-up becomes more reliable, and leadership gets cleaner numbers because stages/tags mean the same thing across the team.

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Define the minimum required fields for quoting and enforce them.
  • Make stages reflect real workflow (intake → quoted → booked → scheduled).
  • Add a definition of done: every lead must have an owner + next action.
  • Run hygiene weekly: dedupe + missing fields + stale lead follow-up.
