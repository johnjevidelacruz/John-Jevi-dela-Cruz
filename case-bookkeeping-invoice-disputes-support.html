Case study

Moving Company: QuickBooks Invoice Tracking + Dispute Support

Billing questions and disputes are operational friction. The goal wasn’t to ‘do accounting’—it was to keep invoicing accurate, track what’s outstanding, and respond to customers quickly with documented context.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Ops Support (billing support)
  • Stack: QuickBooks Online, email/SMS
  • Deliverables: Invoice tracking routine, dispute response templates, escalation rules

Key highlights

  • Dispute handling templates + checklist
  • Clear escalation rules for approvals
  • Documentation-first workflow

Context

At Dan The Moving Man, I handled customer invoice questions and disputes alongside invoice tracking/A/R support.

Objective

Resolve disputes quickly while keeping invoice records accurate and the customer experience calm and professional.

Common dispute types

Most disputes fall into a few predictable buckets.

  • Date/time changes, additional labor/materials, or scope changes.
  • Misunderstandings about deposits, payment links, or balance due.
  • Discounts/adjustments not reflected as expected.

Approach (repeatable resolution flow)

I used a consistent process so nothing got lost in email threads and the books stayed correct.

  • Verify source-of-truth details (job notes, quote, invoice line items, payment records).
  • Explain the delta clearly: what changed, why, and how it impacts the total.
  • If adjustment is approved, document it and update the invoice record cleanly.
  • Close the loop: confirm customer understanding and record the resolution outcome.

Deliverables

Artifacts that reduce future disputes and speed up resolution.

  • Standard email/SMS explanations for common dispute patterns.
  • Clear internal notes on what was approved and by whom.
  • Clean A/R tracking so outstanding balances reflect reality.

Results

Disputes were handled faster with less back-and-forth, and invoice tracking remained usable for follow-up and reporting.

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Anchor every dispute to source documents (quote, job notes, payment record).
  • Explain the delta in plain language before talking about payment.
  • Document approvals and adjustments immediately in the system of record.
  • Track dispute status so you don’t lose follow-ups.
