What I actually do

I sit across ops, CRM, executive support, QA, and bookkeeping so you don’t have to juggle four different part-time hires.

Ops & CRM systems

I design and run the workflows that turn enquiries into scheduled work—without losing leads or creating reporting chaos.

  • Lead intake + follow-up sequences (email/SMS templates, tasks, reminders).
  • CRM hygiene rules: required fields, dedupe, stage definitions, owner alerts.
  • Missed-call recovery and after-hours capture so you don’t pay for leads you never call back.
  • KPI snapshots: answer rate, quote→book, show rate, capacity, and revenue inputs.

Executive & admin support

I remove the “admin drag” that slows teams down by owning communication, documentation, and coordination.

  • Inbox triage and response drafting with clear priorities.
  • Calendar and meeting prep so decisions are captured and acted on.
  • SOPs and templates for repeatable tasks (intake, quoting, disputes, QA).
  • Subscription/payment reminders and vendor coordination to prevent lapses.

Bookkeeping & finance ops support

I keep the books current and close-ready in QBO/Xero—setup, invoicing, bills, matching, and reconciliations—so you can trust your numbers.
