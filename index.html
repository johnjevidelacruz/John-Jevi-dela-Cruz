The operating problem

Work should not stall because the owner is the only person who knows what happens next.

Tasks need chasing. The same decision is made differently by different people. Training happens verbally. Exceptions disappear between inboxes, trackers and tools.

The problem is rarely a lack of effort. It is a lack of an operating system people can follow—one with clear ownership, written standards and visible control points.

How I help

Manage the work. Systemise the process. Control the result.

Technology supports the model; it is not the service. The work starts with the business problem and the people responsible for solving it.

How the work changes

From “ask the person who knows” to “follow the way the business works.”

  1. Diagnose

    See how the work really moves, including handoffs and exceptions.

  2. Assign

    Give each decision and next action a clear owner.

  3. Document

    Capture the standard in practical SOPs, rules and examples.

  4. Make visible

    Build status views, checks and exception reporting.

  5. Coach

    Help people adopt the standard and correct recurring gaps.

  6. Improve

    Use evidence to refine the process instead of relying on opinion.
