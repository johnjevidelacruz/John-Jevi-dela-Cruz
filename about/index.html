About

I understand the work on paper and in practice.

Frontline work taught me where instructions fail. Quality leadership taught me how to coach from evidence. Finance and practice management taught me why accurate status, careful checks, and reliable handoffs matter.

How I think about the work

Make it clear. Make it repeatable. Make exceptions visible.

I am most useful when a function needs both day-to-day ownership and a better way of working. That means understanding how people actually execute the process, then improving the standard without losing the practical details that keep work moving.

“My job is to make the work clearer, easier to repeat, and less dependent on constant checking.”

Operating principles

Simple rules that keep work dependable.

Make ownership clear.

Every priority, handoff, and escalation should have an accountable next person.

Document what helps.

SOPs should answer real questions and make the next task easier to complete.

Keep exceptions visible.

Problems should surface early enough to solve, not disappear until they become urgent.

Start with the recurring problem
