Services

One partner across ops, CRM, executive support, and bookkeeping.

I act as a fractional operations and CRM systems partner who also covers executive support and bookkeeping workflows. Instead of hiring three different people, you get one operator who lives in the tools and owns the process.

Core lanes

Everything I do fits into one of these four lanes, often overlapping in a single engagement.

Ops & CRM systems

Workflow design, automation, and CRM hygiene so leads don’t fall through the cracks and reporting becomes trustworthy.

  • Lead intake → quote → book flow (templates, required fields, follow-ups, handoffs).
  • Pipeline hygiene: dedupe rules, status definitions, tagging standards, and “definition of done” for each stage.
  • Automations that save time (missed-call recovery, reminders, task creation, owner alerts, lightweight integrations).
  • Weekly snapshot reporting that leadership can actually use (answer rate, quote→book, schedule capacity, revenue inputs).

Typical outcome: fewer dropped leads, cleaner handoffs, and faster execution with less manual chasing.

Executive & admin support

Back-office execution and coordination that keeps decisions moving and reduces context-switching for founders and managers.

  • Inbox triage + response drafting with clear priorities and next actions.
  • Calendar management, scheduling, and meeting prep notes so calls have outcomes, not just attendance.
  • SOPs, templates, and “single source of truth” docs for repeatable work (intake, quoting, QA, billing questions).
  • Vendor/contractor coordination (creatives/SEO, tools subscriptions, renewals) with lightweight reporting.

Typical outcome: fewer bottlenecks and less founder time spent on reminders, follow-ups, and re-explaining processes.

QA & reporting

Quality systems and reporting that turn day-to-day activity into measurable improvements.

  • QA scorecards (call/chat/email) with calibrations so scoring is consistent across reviewers.
  • Coaching loops: audit → feedback → action plan → re-audit, tracked in a simple spreadsheet/dashboard.
  • Weekly insights: top failure modes, root-cause patterns, and targeted fixes (scripts, macros, training).
  • Dashboards in Excel/Sheets to track quality, productivity, and operational KPIs without noisy data.

Typical outcome: higher quality scores and fewer recurring mistakes because issues are identified, coached, and verified.

Bookkeeping (QBO/Xero)

Bookkeeping execution that keeps your books current: setup, invoicing, bills, matching, and reconciliations—so month-end isn’t a scramble.

  • QBO/Xero setup from scratch (COA, settings, customers/vendors, products/services, bank feeds).
  • Customer invoicing + payment matching (A/R tracking) and vendor bill entry (A/P tracking).
  • Transaction categorisation/matching with clear rules and a clean suspense process.
  • Monthly bank/credit-card reconciliations and close-prep package (P&L / Balance Sheet for owner/CPA review).

Scope note: bookkeeping + close-prep. Tax filing and tax strategy stay with your CPA/tax pro.

FAQ

Straight answers to the questions founders usually ask before outsourcing ops or bookkeeping.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to the tools (or screenshares), a quick walkthrough of the current process, and clarity on the single outcome you want first (e.g., “reduce missed leads” or “close books monthly on time”).

Do you replace an accountant/CPA?

No. I do bookkeeping execution and close-prep. Your accountant/CPA owns tax filing, compliance, and advisory.

Do you work with messy data?

Yes—most engagements start with cleanup. The first step is always defining what “clean” means: required fields, naming standards, and a reconciliation path for exceptions.
