Case study

Moving Company: Executive Reporting + Cross-team Coordination

Leadership needed clean visibility: bookings, pending quotes, schedule capacity, and issues needing attention—without spending their day chasing updates across tools.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Ops Support / Executive Support
  • Stack: Supermove, QuickBooks Time, Google Sheets/Docs, email
  • Deliverables: Daily snapshot, weekly rollup, comms triage routine

Key highlights

  • Daily + weekly reporting cadence
  • Clear escalation and follow-up ownership
  • Noise reduction via comms triage

Context

In fast-moving small businesses, leaders drown in fragmented updates: ops, sales, marketing, and tools subscriptions all competing for attention.

Objective

Create a weekly operating rhythm and reporting that reduces noise and makes priorities clear.

Approach

I acted as an execution layer: consolidate inputs, produce decision-ready updates, and coordinate follow-through.

  • Generated daily/weekly sales and ops reports with consistent definitions.
  • Coordinated marketing team outputs (creatives/SEO) and routed feedback efficiently.
  • Maintained subscriptions/payment reminders to prevent tool lapses.
  • Documented recurring processes and created templates so work didn’t depend on one person’s memory.

Deliverables

Artifacts that make leadership communication easier.

  • Weekly summary format: what changed, blockers, decisions needed, next actions.
  • Basic KPI sheet and cadence for updates.
  • Templates for recurring comms (handoffs, reminders, escalations).

Results

Leaders spend less time chasing updates and more time making decisions because the work is packaged into consistent, action-based summaries.

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Standardize KPI definitions once; don’t reinvent them weekly.
  • Summaries should end with decisions needed + owners + due dates.
  • Route updates into one system (not scattered across chats).
  • Create templates for recurring messages to reduce cognitive load.
