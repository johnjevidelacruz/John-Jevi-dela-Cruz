For moving & logistics teams

Moving company CRM and operations specialist.

I help moving and logistics companies stop losing leads between phones, email, and CRM. I align Supermove, RingCentral, scheduling, and your accounting tools so every enquiry, job, and invoice moves cleanly through the system.

Who this is for

  • Moving and logistics owners and ops managers
  • Teams using Supermove, RingCentral, or similar tools
  • Businesses that need fewer missed leads and fewer job mistakes

Common problems I’m brought in to solve

A moving company has a lot of moving parts. When CRM, phones, and scheduling aren’t aligned, you feel it in missed revenue and rework.

Leads going missing

Calls and enquiries don’t always make it into Supermove or your CRM. I standardise intake, tagging, and status flows so every lead is captured, tracked, and followed up.

Jobs not matching what was sold

When stages, notes, and job details are inconsistent, crews show up under-informed. I clean up job pipelines and workflows so operations see the same story sales promised.

Phones and CRM not talking

I help align RingCentral call flows, SMS templates, and Supermove so missed calls, voicemails, and texts are logged and acted on instead of disappearing.

Invoices and payments out of sync

I connect your job pipeline with Xero or QuickBooks Online so invoices and payments reflect what was actually booked and delivered, not just what someone remembers.

Deliverables you receive

Concrete outputs you can keep using after the engagement—configured inside your tools and documented in plain language.

CRM hygiene pack

Rules and standards so your pipeline stays usable.

  • Stage definitions + required fields + tags
  • Dedupe approach and “definition of done” for intake
  • Templates for follow-up and handoffs

Lead recovery loop

A repeatable system to prevent missed calls turning into missed revenue.

  • Missed-call checklist and escalation rules
  • First-touch message templates (SMS/email)
  • Owner alerts for urgent/high-value leads

Ops-to-books alignment

Reduce billing errors by tying job reality to invoices.

  • Invoice trigger rules (when to invoice, what to include)
  • Payment matching/deposit notes
  • Exceptions process for adjustments and disputes

30-day sprint plan

If you want fast improvement, this is the typical sequence (adjusted to your stack and constraints).

Week 1: Map + clean

Get clarity and stop the bleeding.

  • Walkthrough and pain-point map
  • Fix obvious stage/tag chaos
  • Define minimum intake data

Week 2: Automate + template

Reduce manual chasing.

  • Follow-up sequences and templates
  • Missed-call recovery flow
  • Owner alert rules

Week 3: Reporting + coaching

Make the system measurable.

  • KPI snapshot (answer rate, quote→book, backlog)
  • QA spot checks on intake quality
  • Adjust scripts/templates based on failures

Week 4: Handoff + stabilization

Make it stick.

  • Short SOPs and checklists
  • Backlog of next improvements
  • Team handoff and Q&A

What working together looks like

We start small, prove the value inside your moving operation, and then decide how deep you want to go.

1 · System walkthrough

You walk me through how leads come in today, how jobs are booked, and how they show up in your scheduling and accounting tools. We map where things fall through the cracks.
