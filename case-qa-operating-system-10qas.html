Case study

Support Vendor: QA Operating System for 10 Quality Analysts

A QA program can run a high audit volume and still fail if coverage is uneven and insights don’t reach the right stakeholders. This engagement focused on the QA ‘operating system’: schedules, routines, and reporting.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Lead QA (managed 10 QAs)
  • Stack: Power BI, Excel/Sheets, client reporting decks
  • Deliverables: QA schedules, weekly insights pack, routine calibrations

Key highlights

  • Coverage-aligned QA scheduling
  • Weekly trend insights, not just scores
  • Calibration routine for consistency

Context

At iQor, I led a QA team of 10 and owned scheduling, auditing coverage, calibrations, and weekly insights reporting for leadership and clients.

Objective

Build a QA operating system that scales: consistent scoring, predictable coverage, and a coaching loop that produces measurable improvement.

Approach

I built structure first, then tightened feedback loops.

  • Created QA schedules aligned to agent teams and priority segments (e.g., bottom performers).
  • Led calibrations to keep scoring consistent across sites and partner vendors.
  • Tracked coaching compliance and audit volumes to ensure the program ran as designed.
  • Prepared weekly insights decks connecting failure modes to recommended actions.

Deliverables

Repeatable QA mechanics.

  • QA scheduling model and coverage targets.
  • Scorecard guidance notes and calibration log.
  • Weekly insights deck template with charts and action items.
  • Coaching compliance tracker.

Results

More consistent scoring and faster improvement cycles because coaching and re-audits were planned, tracked, and reviewed.

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Separate coverage (audits) from improvement (coaching + re-audit). Track both.
  • Calibrate regularly; publish scoring notes so edge cases don’t drift.
  • Focus on the top 3 failure modes per week—fixing everything at once doesn’t work.
  • Make every action measurable (who coached, what changed, what improved).
