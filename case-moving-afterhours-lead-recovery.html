Case study

Moving Company: After-hours Lead Recovery from Missed Calls

Missed calls were being captured by an AI system, but they still required a human to turn recordings into actionable leads. The gap was speed and consistency.

← Back to case studies

At a glance

  • Role: Ops Support
  • Stack: RingCentral, Supermove CRM, email/SMS
  • Deliverables: Missed-call processing SOP, lead creation rules, message templates

Key highlights

  • Fast conversion of recordings → CRM leads
  • Templates that focus on missing fields only
  • Clear ownership and prioritisation tags

Context

After-hours missed calls are common in moving/logistics—and those missed leads are expensive if they aren’t recovered quickly.

Objective

Turn missed-call recordings into complete leads in the CRM and trigger fast follow-up, so after-hours enquiries still convert.

Approach

I used a repeatable “listen → extract → create lead → outreach” flow and standardized the data captured.

  • Reviewed call recordings, captured move details, and created leads in the CRM.
  • Sent first-touch outreach (email/SMS) requesting missing details and offering scheduling options.
  • Routed high-value or urgent jobs to a salesperson with clear notes and next steps.
  • Tracked outcomes (contacted, responded, quoted, booked) to validate the workflow.

Deliverables

A lead-recovery operating loop that can run daily.

  • Missed-call handling checklist + minimum info capture template.
  • CRM lead creation and assignment rules.
  • First-touch message templates and escalation rules.
  • Tracking for recovered leads and conversion status.

Results

Fewer leads went cold overnight, and the team had a clear process instead of relying on memory or “checking when there’s time.”

Reusable checklist

If you’re doing something similar, this is the short checklist I follow to keep the work clean and close-ready.

  • Check missed calls at a fixed cadence (e.g., first thing in the morning + mid-day).
  • Create the lead immediately in the CRM—even if details are incomplete.
  • Send first-touch outreach within the same day.
  • Escalate urgent/high-value cases to a salesperson with context and a proposed next action.
