How I help

I manage the work today and improve how it runs tomorrow.

My work sits across people, process and systems. The goal is simple: clear ownership, consistent execution and fewer problems returning to the owner.

Make work repeatable

Turn repeated explanations into a process the team can use.

What I take ownership of
